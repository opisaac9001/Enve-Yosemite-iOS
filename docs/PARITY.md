# Feature status

The app is in development. “Implemented” describes available behavior, not certification for field navigation. Content counts and official rules are dated snapshots; individual records show their sources.

| Area | Available | Dependency or limit |
|---|---|---|
| Maps | One continuous offline map on the Map tab, from the whole park down to Valley buildings and campsite numbers, with no mode switch. USGS 3DEP shaded relief and elevation tint over the whole park (about 30 m), coarse 3DEP context around it and the 20 m Valley grid on top; NPS roads, trails, buildings, parking and points of interest park-wide; USGS NHD rivers, named creeks, perennial streams, lakes and reservoirs; USGS GNIS summit, pass, meadow and canyon names; park boundary; contours (50 m in the Valley, 200 m elsewhere); shuttle routes, categories, grouped markers, numbered campsites in every campground, trailheads, campgrounds and region points beyond the Valley, saved pins, parked car, search, finder for regions, park hikes and campgrounds, fit park / fit Valley, layers, sun preview, key and sources. NHD water is bundled for the four HU8 basins around the park (Upper Merced, Upper Tuolumne, Upper San Joaquin, Mono), so the far corners of the surround have relief but no streams. Park-wide NPS supplements were fetched October 30, 2026, the Valley packs September 21; NPS positional accuracy is mostly not recorded. A drawn line does not mean a road or trail is open. Each hike carries the NPS distance, gain and time where published, the stated starting point, an NPS GIS trailhead point and dated sources. Difficulty appears only where NPS rates the hike; others read "Not rated by NPS" and stay out of the difficulty filters.

Park-pack traces state what they approximate. Destinations without an NPS point are feature ends or the trail point nearest a lake shoreline or POI, labelled approximate: McGurk Meadow, Sentinel Dome, Wawona Point, Wapama Falls, Smith Peak, Poopenaut Valley, May Lake, Lukens Lake, North Dome, Mono Pass and Glen Aulin. Wawona Point and Guardians start about 30 m from the trailhead point, and Smith Peak from the entrance about 15 m. The Grizzly Giant, Guardians and Tuolumne Grove loops have no NPS walking direction, and the Merced Grove fork sits at the trailhead in the NPS lines rather than half a mile in. Guardians, Tuolumne Grove and the Dog Lake & Lembert Dome out-and-back are stored as closed walks so their travelled distance is right; the stats and variant picker show the published "round trip" wording, while the route sequence card heads them "Loop". Every chain is in the [route atlas](hiking-atlas/README.md).

Remaining gaps:
- Not listed: the Laurel, Vernon and Rancheria loop, whose starting point the NPS page does not state, overnight wilderness routes, and winter snowshoe and ski trails.
- The Elizabeth Lake and Cathedral Lakes starts follow the NPS day-hike page, which names the visitor center. NPS GIS maps separate trailheads; the difference is shown on each hike.
- Pothole Dome uses the NPS shuttle-stop point because NPS maps no separate parking point.
- Notices are matched to hikes by curated patterns, distinctive route names and the roads the trailheads are on. A match is a name match, not a ruling on access. Valley hikes have no access-road matching.
- Hike accessibility details come only from dated NPS statements. Most park hikes have none and say so.

## Known limits

- Bundled base packs are updated with the app. Repairing a damaged bundled resource requires reinstalling or updating the app.
- Optional GIS downloads keep the previous valid pack on failure, but do not resume partial downloads.
- Building callouts use centroid anchors. Relief covers the park bounds at about 30 m, a ≈250 m surround and a ≈800 m outer tier reaching about 300 km out, enough for the most zoomed-out view on every supported phone and iPad layout, including landscape phones and one-third split view. Only chrome leaving a band thinner than about 12 % of the screen width (for example under 103 pt on an 844 pt landscape phone) stops zoom-out at the terrain edge, so the whole park no longer fits clear of that chrome. Far out, the Pacific and the bays show as flat sea-level ground with no coastline, and open ocean beyond 3DEP coverage is blank.
- Debug builds take several seconds to prepare the park relief on first open (about 0.4 s in an optimised build on a Mac); the map shows a loading state meanwhile.
- Caltrans chain-control URLs are configurable; the upstream returned errors during initial verification. An unavailable feed is displayed as unavailable.
- Live feeds, notification delivery, physical-device GPS and long offline field use have not been verified end to end.
- There is no GPS recording, track history, off-route alert, custom route planner or background navigation service. The passive route figures compare a fix only with the selected route's mapped NPS line; they say nothing about the trail's condition or whether the walker is on it.
- Trailhead sun times assume a flat horizon at the trailhead point. Ridges, cliffs, forest and weather are not modelled.
